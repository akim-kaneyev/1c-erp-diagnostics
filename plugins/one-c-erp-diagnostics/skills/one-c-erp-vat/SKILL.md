# VAT

Separate operation date, source-document date, tax-record date and declaration period. Drill the book/register entry to its actual registrar. Check for original and corrective entries coexisting across periods. Compare VAT and accounting contours without assuming they must coincide.

For closed periods, first identify the standard accounting mechanism available to the user; never default to reopening a period.

Current tax law, FNS guidance and 1C methodology must be verified against current official sources before being used as normative facts.
