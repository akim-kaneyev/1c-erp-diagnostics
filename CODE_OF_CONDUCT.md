# Code of Conduct

This project aims to maintain a professional, evidence-focused technical community.

Expected behavior:
- discuss claims and evidence rather than attacking people;
- disclose uncertainty and limitations;
- respect confidentiality and intellectual property;
- avoid publishing customer/company data or credentials;
- give actionable, technically specific review feedback;
- correct mistakes openly when evidence changes.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory behavior, doxxing, credential sharing and deliberate disclosure of confidential data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users or confidential information.
